Golf groups

You get: groups of any size for every round, so people play with new faces and repeat as little as possible. Works for golf trips, leagues, card nights and mixers. Free, no sign-up, and your list never leaves this browser.

This is a filled-in example: 16 players, groups of 4, 5 rounds. The groups are below. Type your own names, or change the numbers, and press Make the groups.
