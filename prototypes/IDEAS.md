## fair-rota: volunteer groups build rotas by hand around who can't come
- Who has it: the person who makes the rota for a church, club or small team
- In their words: "taking into account the days they are not available" ([source](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/discussions/excelgeneral/how-to-set-up-volunteer-rota/183931)), "a spreadsheet can be made that will allocate these shifts out" ([source](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5075313/create-an-excel-document-to-work-out-shifts))
- Independent sources: 3 (also [Chandoo forum](https://chandoo.org/forum/threads/allocate-duties-by-availability-and-role.45964/))
- Smallest useful thing: one offline page that shares turns evenly, skips away days, and prints the rota.
- Pass mark: 5 people say it made a real rota for their group, by 2026-11-21
- Status: live
