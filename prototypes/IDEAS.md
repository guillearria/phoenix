Problems real people describe in public, each with its evidence and a pass mark. Newest at the bottom.

## golf-groups: Golf groups want new foursomes each round without repeat partners
- Who has it: people who organize golf trips, weekly leagues and other group games
- In their words: "pair everyone with everyone else at least once in 5 rounds" ([source](https://thesandtrap.com/forums/topic/64321-golf-pairings-software)), "without playing with the same person multiple times" ([source](http://mathcentral.uregina.ca/QQ/database/QQ.09.15/h/jeff2.html)), "ultimately get to play in groups that are unique" ([source](http://mathcentral.uregina.ca/QQ/database/QQ.09.08/h/d1.html))
- Independent sources: 13 (12 separate askers on [Math Central](https://mathcentral.uregina.ca/QandQ/topics/golf%2020) for 20 players alone, 2004 to 2015, plus The Sand Trap; a [seller](https://probablegolfinstruction.com/tournament-pairings-sample-spreadsheet.html) charges $12 to $45 for spreadsheets)
- Smallest useful thing: a page that takes names, group size and rounds, and prints groups with as few repeats as possible.
- Pass mark: 10 people use it for a real event by 2026-11-21
- Status: live
