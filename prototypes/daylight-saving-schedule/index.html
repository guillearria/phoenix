What this is, and what comes next

Twice a year parents ask the same thing: shift a bit each day, or all at once? Guides answer with one sample table for a 7 pm bedtime. This page builds the table from your own times.

Already out there: printed charts from baby brands, and a time change helper inside one sleep gadget's app. This page needs no gadget or app, takes every time in your day, and prints.

Next, if people ask: two children on different routines, half before and half after, a calendar file to import.
